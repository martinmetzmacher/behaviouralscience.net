About this Blog

with 3 comments

The Behavioural Science Blog is about high-quality and comprehensive articles written for those who want to follow the latest research, but find traditional sources inaccessible. Every who has got something to say about Behavioural Science is invited to join in and become an author.

At the moment there are two author who have posted more than one article:

  • Martin Metzmacher &
  • Christina Summerdyk

This Blog has also featured a guest writer:

  • Unconscious Perception: Reply by Tom Wootton

The people reading this site are mostly students or “others” – yeah I know bad research (-:

The readers of this blog prefer to read text, also like to have graphics in it and from time to time they enjoy a video or audio posting. And that is what they find on this blog – I guess that is the reason, why this blog has about 25 35 RSS readers – more to come!

I could go on and on about this blog, but I suggest you rather have a look yourself. Please tell us what you think and which topic interest you most! If you really want to know more about this blog, I suggest you read the article about the Behavioural Science Blog development.
